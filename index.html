Who We Are

Engineering a clearer view of the world.

CadLab Consulting and Research Pvt. Ltd. is an Odisha-based company established in 2020. Our work spans UAV/drone mapping, LiDAR and geospatial solutions, architectural design, 3D modelling and Building Information Modelling (BIM).

We combine data acquisition, spatial analysis and technical modelling to support planning and development across infrastructure, urban, industrial, agricultural and environmental applications.

  • UAV & LiDAR mapping
  • GIS & remote sensing
  • Architectural design
  • 3D modelling & BIM
